## Voice Direction & Technical Notes

### Pacing Guidelines:

**0:00 - 0:40** (Introduction & Basic Fields)
- **Pace:** Moderate (150 WPM)
- **Energy:** Welcoming, instructional
- **Emphasis:** "instantly," "expert guidance," "important"

**0:40 - 1:08** (Complex Fields)
- **Pace:** Slightly slower (140 WPM) for emphasis
- **Energy:** More enthusiastic, highlighting features
- **Emphasis:** "really shines," "explains," "implications"

**1:08 - 1:43** (Detailed Examples & Close)
- **Pace:** Moderate, building to confident close (150-160 WPM)
- **Energy:** Confident, reassuring, strong CTA
- **Emphasis:** "clarifies," "invaluable," "confidence," "stress-free"

### Vocal Characteristics:

- **Gender:** Neutral (works for any voice)
- **Accent:** Clear, neutral English (Indian or International)
- **Age Range:** 30s-40s (mature, trustworthy)
- **Tone:** Professional instructor, friendly expert
- **Delivery Style:** Conversational yet authoritative

### Pause Points:

- After "instantly activates" (0.5s pause)
- After "important warning" (0.5s pause)
- After "updates instantly" (0.3s pause)
- After "really shines" (0.5s pause)
- Before final CTA (0.7s pause for impact)

## Screen Recording Tips for Sync

### Cursor Movement:
- Move cursor deliberately, not too fast
- Pause briefly on each field before clicking (0.5s)
- Hover over key recommendations in panel

### Timing Markers:
- **00:08** - Click Given Name field (wait for panel to load)
- **00:17** - Click Surname field
- **00:28** - Quick selections (gender, aliases)
- **00:33** - Date of Birth entry visible
- **00:53** - Employment Type dropdown moment
- **01:08** - Visible mark field highlight
- **01:20** - Aadhaar field interaction

### Visual Highlights:
- **Green highlight** on active field should be clearly visible
- **Panel animations** should complete before voiceover describes them
- **Recommendations** should be on screen long enough to read key points

### Sound Effects (Optional):
- Subtle "click" sound on field selection (very quiet, -30dB)

## Post-Production Checklist

### Audio Quality:
- [ ] Clean recording, no background noise
- [ ] Consistent volume levels throughout
- [ ] Clear pronunciation of "FormWise" and technical terms
- [ ] Natural breathing, no awkward pauses
- [ ] Peak levels at -6dB

### Sync with Video:
- [ ] Voiceover matches visual actions within 0.2s
- [ ] Pauses align with field clicks and panel updates
- [ ] Key phrases sync with highlighted features on screen
- [ ] CTA timing matches final screen hold

### Music & SFX:
- [ ] Background music doesn't compete with voice
- [ ] Music fades at appropriate points
- [ ] Sound effects (if used) are subtle and professional
- [ ] No audio clipping or distortion

### Export Settings:
- [ ] Format: MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio)
- [ ] Resolution: 1920x1080 (1080p)
- [ ] Frame Rate: 30fps
- [ ] Audio: 48kHz, 320kbps AAC stereo
- [ ] Bitrate: 8-10 Mbps (video)
