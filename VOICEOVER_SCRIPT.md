## Voice Direction Notes

### Tone Guidance for Each Section:

1. **Opening (0:00-0:05):**
   - Start empathetic, acknowledging the pain point
   - Slight pause before "But it doesn't have to be" for impact
   - Shift to optimistic tone

2. **Value Proposition (0:05-0:12):**
   - Confident and clear
   - Emphasize "instant" and "intelligent"
   - Warm, reassuring delivery

3. **Features (0:12-0:18):**
   - Professional and credible
   - Emphasize "10,000 users" for social proof
   - Stress "100% private" as a key trust factor

4. **How It Works (0:18-0:25):**
   - Simplified, friendly tone
   - Counting cadence for the three steps
   - Emphasize "That's it" to show ease

5. **CTA (0:25-0:33):**
   - Energetic and encouraging
   - Emphasize "completely free"
   - Close with confident, memorable tagline

### Pacing:
- **Words per minute:** 140-160 (moderate pace)
- **Pauses:** Strategic 0.5-1 second pauses after key statements
- **Emphasis:** Bold words in script indicate vocal emphasis

### Vocal Characteristics:
- **Gender:** Neutral (works for male or female voice)
- **Accent:** Neutral/Standard (Indian English or International English)
- **Age:** Mid 30s-40s (mature, trustworthy)
- **Energy:** Medium-high (enthusiastic but not over-the-top)

---

## Recording Tips

1. **Warm-up:** Practice the script 2-3 times before recording
2. **Environment:** Quiet room, minimal echo
3. **Microphone Distance:** 6-8 inches from microphone
4. **Breath Control:** Take breaths at natural pauses (marked with line breaks)
5. **Smile While Speaking:** Especially during the CTA - it comes through in the voice
6. **Multiple Takes:** Record 3-5 complete takes, choose the best
7. **Edit Points:** Mark where visual transitions happen for easier editing

## Music Recommendations

### Background Music Style:
- **Genre:** Corporate, Inspiring, Technology
- **Mood:** Uplifting, Professional, Modern
- **Tempo:** 110-130 BPM
- **Instruments:** Light synths, subtle piano, soft percussion
- **Volume:** -20dB to -25dB (behind voiceover, not competing)

### Suggested Tracks (Royalty-Free):
- "Corporate Inspiring" style tracks
- "Tech Product Demo" background music
- "Modern Business Presentation" themes

### Audio Mixing:
- **Voiceover:** Center channel, -6dB peak
- **Background Music:** Stereo, -20dB, ducked to -25dB during speech
- **Sound Effects:** Optional subtle "whoosh" for transitions (if needed)
- **EQ:** High-pass filter at 80Hz on voiceover, cut muddy frequencies (200-400Hz)
