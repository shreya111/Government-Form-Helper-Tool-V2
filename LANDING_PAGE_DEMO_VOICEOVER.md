## Recording & Production Guide

### Pre-Recording Checklist:

- [ ] Script reviewed and practiced 3-5 times
- [ ] Difficult pronunciations noted (FormWise, bureaucracy)
- [ ] Emphasis points marked in personal copy
- [ ] Water at hand (stay hydrated)
- [ ] Recording environment tested (minimal echo)
- [ ] Microphone positioned correctly (6-8 inches)
- [ ] Recording software set up (48kHz, 24-bit WAV)

### During Recording:

1. **Warm-up:** Read script aloud 2x before recording
2. **Multiple Takes:** Record at least 3 complete takes
3. **Section Backups:** Record 2-3 versions of CTA section (most critical)
4. **Natural Breathing:** Don't hold breath; breathe at marked pauses
5. **Smile Voice:** Especially important for "completely free" and closing
6. **Energy Maintenance:** Keep energy consistent throughout

### Post-Recording:

1. **Selection:** Choose best complete take or composite best sections
2. **Editing:**
   - Remove breaths that are too loud
   - Cut out clicks, pops, and mouth noises
   - Tighten gaps but keep natural pauses
3. **EQ:**
   - High-pass filter at 80Hz (remove rumble)
   - Slight boost at 3-5kHz (clarity)
   - Cut at 200-400Hz if muddy
4. **Compression:**
   - Ratio: 3:1 to 4:1
   - Threshold: -18dB
   - Attack: 5-10ms, Release: 50-100ms
5. **De-esser:** Reduce sibilance (S sounds) around 6-8kHz
6. **Normalization:** Peak at -3dB to -6dB
7. **Reverb (optional):** Very subtle room ambience (-40dB wet)

### Final Export:

- **Format:** WAV (for video editing) or MP3 (for final delivery)
- **Sample Rate:** 48kHz (matches video standard)
- **Bit Depth:** 24-bit (WAV) or 320kbps (MP3)
- **Loudness:** -16 LUFS integrated (broadcast standard)
- **File Naming:** `FormWise_Landing_VO_v1_Final.wav`
